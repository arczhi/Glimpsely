## 完整提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished minimalist bunny mascot logo for Glimpsely, a caring everyday memory assistant whose personality is "understands you at a glance".
The entire logo is just an adorable lively little rabbit head, slight three-quarter view and a subtle head tilt, as if it has just turned and glanced at the viewer with quiet curiosity. Two long rabbit ears, one upright and one gently bent at the tip. Two small expressive eyes with pupils glancing sideways toward the viewer, clearly communicating a quick knowing peek, not a blank front-facing stare. A tiny simple nose and restrained little smile. Soft rounded cheek silhouette. Characterful, sweet, attentive, nimble. Keep the eyes legible and friendly, never sly or suspicious.
Style: very simple clean monoline outline drawing, smooth rounded ends and subtly organic curves. Economical strokes, moderate line weight, plenty of empty space inside the bunny. Muted sage green outlines, cream bunny interior, optional tiny soft dusty peach inner-ear accent. No heavy solid masses, no fur detailing, no shading, no gradients, no pencil texture, no 3D.
Composition: square avatar, bunny head with ears centered as one compact balanced mark occupying about 70% canvas height, safe generous margins for circular crop. Solid opaque warm ivory background. Professional memorable logo, cute but understated, readable at small avatar sizes.
Strictly no phone, screen, screenshot, document, card, rectangle, device, robot, accessory, text, letters, watermark, stars, extra objects, surrounding frame, body or scenery. Output a single final logo only, no mockup or design board.
