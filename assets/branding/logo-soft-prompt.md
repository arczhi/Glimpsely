# 柔和线条版 Logo

使用内置 image_gen 生成。叠放截图代表记忆，笑脸代表生活助手，暖黄色星光代表主动推荐。

## 完整提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a single finished minimalist logo for Glimpsely, a friendly personal everyday-life memory assistant on WeChat: it remembers the content of screenshots you send and proactively offers useful recommendations.
A new visual direction: clean soft-colored monoline illustration, not pencil sketch.
Design one compact integrated emblem: two softly rounded overlapping screenshot cards, the front card becoming a friendly little assistant with two tiny eyes and a subtle smile. The rear card is visible only as a simple offset contour, suggesting stored memories. One small four-point warm apricot-gold sparkle sits near the upper right of the front card, expressing a timely helpful suggestion. The screenshot/card silhouette must read immediately. Keep details extremely spare; no text rows, no extra objects. Warm, attentive, calm, quietly clever personality.
Muted sage-green outlines with smooth rounded stroke caps, gently organic contours, thick enough for a 48px avatar. Front card pale cream, rear card a very subtle pale sage fill. Small warm apricot sparkle. Solid opaque warm ivory background across the entire canvas. Flat pastel fills, no gradients or shadows. Strong enough contrast despite the soft palette.
Square canvas, one centered unified symbol occupying 65-70 percent of width, ample balanced whitespace and safe circular cropping. Professional iconic simplicity. No typography, letters, watermark, robot antenna, brain, gears, circuit, bells, extra decoration, mockup, presentation board, 3D, texture or pencil grain. Output only the finished avatar image.
